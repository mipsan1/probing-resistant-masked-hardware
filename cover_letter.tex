\textbf{Title:} Probing-Resistant Masked Hardware: Formal Security Proofs and Simulation-Based Side-Channel Verification

Dear Editor,

Following the editorial notice of 9 October 2026, the appendix previously uploaded as a separate file has been relabeled as \emph{Supplemental Material} and is uploaded as such. The main manuscript is self-contained and is at most 13 pages in the two-column IEEE format.

\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.1em
\item A characterization of the soundness gap between equipment-free value-based statistical verification and glitch-extended probing, via a cone-non-completeness condition; a first-order lint shown sound, and an explicit first-order witness showing that the gap is real.
\item A masked multiplication gadget (DOM with register barrier) proven robust $d$-NI with perfect simulation for arbitrary $d$, and a first-order AES S-box built from it.
\item A reproducible, equipment-free verification flow for the first-order design combining the lint, PROLEAD and gate-level TVLA/MI, calibrated with an unmasked positive control.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Scope.} The empirical evaluation targets the first-order design; the gadget-level proof holds for arbitrary $d$, and the lint theorem is stated at first order. Limitations are discussed in the paper.

\textbf{Supplemental Material.} Corrected PROLEAD worst-case value, the explicit gap witness, the pre-barrier PROLEAD history, extended discussion, further verifiers and full-chip details.

\textbf{Artifact.} RTL, synthesis scripts, lint, PROLEAD configurations and results are available at \url{https://github.com/mipsan1/probing-resistant-masked-hardware} and in the artifact bundle accompanying the submission.

The manuscript is not published elsewhere or under consideration at another journal. All authors approve this submission. No conflict of interest.
